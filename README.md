# 🔴 React

## You're stuck. Again.

In a game. In YouTube. In a conversation that should have ended. In a scroll that brings nothing but fatigue.

You know there are things you could do. Good things. Things that actually make you happy. But your brain — it's like a full hard drive. **You can't remember a single one.**

You've tried todo lists. They're endless. They crush you. They remind you of what you *should* do, not what you *want* to do.

You've tried reminders. They annoy you.

You've tried habit trackers. They turn life into a spreadsheet.

And every time — the same thing. Some kind of **amnesia** cuts off your ability to switch. You stay stuck, even though deep down you know there's another way.

---

## React is not another planner.

It's the **red pill** for when your brain freezes.

No "how are you?"
No scheduled reminders.
No endless lists.

You just open it — and see **4 actions**. The ones you've chosen before when you felt exactly like this.

👇 Tap. The app closes. You go do it.

🎲 Don't like the set? Shuffle.
➕ Missing something? Add your own.

The app doesn't ask questions. It doesn't ask "did it help?" It just **remembers** what you choose. And next time, it shows those more often.

---

## Who is this for?

For someone who:

- Has tried every way to "get started"
- Is tired of long lists that only make them feel worse
- Knows deep down there's a desire to do things — but can't remember *what*
- Watches hours, days, weeks disappear into mindless scrolling or gaming
- Doesn't want to "become productive" — they want to **remember how to enjoy what they do**

---

## How it works (short)

1. Open the app
2. See 4 actions
3. Tap one
4. App closes — you do it

The more often you choose an action, the higher its chance to appear again.

**Long press** anywhere → statistics (your top actions)
**Export** → JSON / CSV (share to Telegram, email, or feed to AI)
